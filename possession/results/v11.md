# v11: two gated experiments on top of v8 (IDSSE TUNE LOMO, seeds 20-24)

Protocol (both): strict LOMO over J03WMX J03WN1 J03WOH J03WOY J03WPY, seeds 20-24, v8 decoder (0.5*TCN logp + 0.5*eR, viterbi_scaled(e,20,5,1-0.6b)),
scored with possession/score.py vs merged truth (K 2, primary) and raw DFL truth (info). v8 reference vs merged: 0.7563 [.763 .719 .786 .734 .779]; vs DFL 0.6517.
Gate (fixed): mean vs merged >= 0.7663 AND > v8 on >= 4/5 matches. Single-match differences < 0.04 are noise. J03WQQ / J03WR9 untouched.

## E2 design (fixed before running students)
- Teacher: v6 recipe (TCN4, 1500 steps, SHORT_W 3, boundary head, EMA, mirror/swap aug, IDSSE only) on CLEAN features (tcn_feats_clean.pkl), merged (K 2) labels, seed 20.
- Cross-fitting: one teacher per unordered pair {m,k} (10 teachers), trained on the other 3 TUNE matches. It predicts (mirror TTA, 3-class log-probs) for k and m.
  Student for held-out m uses, for each training match k, the soft labels of the teacher excluding {m,k}. So no teacher/soft label has seen m, and none has seen the match it labels.
- Student: v8 recipe on degraded canon + 6 DR variants of the 4 training matches; same time axis (checked: clean/degraded/DR shapes identical).
- Loss = CE(merged labels, 3 class, SHORT_W weights) + 0.5 * T^2 * KL(p_teacher^T || p_student^T) with T = 2 (standard KD direction and T^2 scaling; weights fixed, not tuned) + 0.5 * boundary BCE (unchanged).
  Teacher soft labels follow augmentation: pitch mirror keeps them, team swap permutes channels H<->A.
- Student seeds 20-24, teacher seed 20 only (cost bound). Code possession/v11_distill.py, scoring possession/v11_eval.py (arg D).

## E1 design
possession/v11_ext.py = v8_train.py + VAR. V1 = IDSSE + Metrica games 1-3, V2 = + SkillCorner. IDSSE labels merged K 2 (as v8); Metrica labels (5 Hz) merged with K 2 s = 10 steps;
SkillCorner team-only CE as in v4 (D masked, -1 unknown), unmerged (no D class, merge undefined). Sampling mass 0.60/0.15/0.25 renormalised (V1: 0.8/0.2).

## Verdict
No variant passes the gate; none beats v8 on the mean. V1 equals v8 within noise (-0.0008 merged, +0.003 DFL); adding SkillCorner hurts (-0.012); distillation hurts (-0.016, 0/5 wins).
Metrica merging did not make external data useful. Not wired, nothing frozen.

## Caveats
- v8 per-match reference values are 3-decimal; wins compared against them.
- SkillCorner labels not merged (team-only, no D). Metrica merge at 5 Hz (K 2 s = 10 steps).
- E2: single teacher seed (20); teachers trained on only 3 matches (less data than the 4-match v6 clean figure), so a weaker teacher than the ~0.72 ceiling may explain the null. KD direction/T^2 scaling are my reading of the spec. Single-match differences < 0.04 are noise; mean differences here are ~0.001-0.016.
